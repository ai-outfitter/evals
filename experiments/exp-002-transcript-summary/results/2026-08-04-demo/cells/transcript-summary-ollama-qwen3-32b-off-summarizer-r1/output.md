## Summary  
AI Outfitter personas, inspired by lean start-up principles, provide a structured framework for aligning design and development efforts with specific user needs. These personas act as "project steering docs" to clarify the target audience during reviews of code, documentation, or design. By defining a persona with attributes like role, biography, and organization, teams can avoid vague decision-making and ensure outputs align with the intended user’s expertise and context (e.g., engineer vs. finance professional). The philosophy emphasizes human-centric design augmented by agents, with personas guiding both humans and AI tools throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key points  
- Personas originate from lean start-up principles and are not fixed but flexible combinations of role, biography, and organization.  
- Personas act as project steering documents, ensuring agents understand the intended audience for a project.  
- Flexibility allows creating personas (e.g., "John Doe as a designer at ACME" vs. "John Doe as a researcher at Weyland Yutah") to test varied perspectives.  
- Purpose extends beyond reviews to inform technical depth and design tone (e.g., engineering vs. non-technical contexts).  
- The philosophy prioritizes human-first design, with agents supporting human decision-making based on persona-defined priorities.
