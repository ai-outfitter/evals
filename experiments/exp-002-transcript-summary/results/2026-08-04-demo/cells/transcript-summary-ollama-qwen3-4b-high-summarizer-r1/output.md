## Summary
AI Outfitter personas—derived from lean startup principles—enable teams to review design changes, code, or documentation from a specific human role's perspective (e.g., engineers or finance professionals). These personas act as project steering documents that clarify the target audience, ensuring the agentic process remains human-centered and avoids technical abstraction by designing for concrete users rather than abstract "who they're building for."

## Key points
- Personas are flat descriptions (role, biography, organization) that can be mixed and matched (e.g., John Doe as a designer at ACMICO or researcher at Weyland, Utah).
- Outfitter provides a review mechanism, but personas function as project steering documents to define the target user even without the tool.
- Personas help specify how project outputs should be presented (e.g., technical details for engineers vs. non-technical explanations for finance personnel).
- The philosophy emphasizes designing for humans first before integrating agents to prevent projects from becoming "amorphous blobs" of undefined targets.
