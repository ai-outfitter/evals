## Summary
AI Outfitter personas are project steering documents inspired by lean startup methodology that help humans and agents understand who they're building for. Composed of a role, biography, and organization, personas enable you to review designs, code, and documentation from a specific perspective, guiding technical tone and ensuring decisions are made for a defined audience rather than an abstract one.

## Key points
- Personas allow review of design changes, code, and documentation from a specific viewpoint
- A persona consists of three components: role, biography, and organization
- Personas enable flexible mixing and matching (e.g., the same person can be a designer at one company or a researcher at another)
- Personas help determine appropriate technical level and communication style (engineer vs. non-technical stakeholder)
- Personas function as project steering documents that help both humans and agents navigate the development process
- The underlying philosophy prioritizes human-centered design with agents integrated into that approach
- Personas guide every phase: spec writing, planning, and review
- Personas prevent designing for an undefined or amorphous audience by anchoring decisions to a specific person
