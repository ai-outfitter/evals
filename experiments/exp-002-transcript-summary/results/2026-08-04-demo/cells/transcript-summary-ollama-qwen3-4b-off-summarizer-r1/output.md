## Summary
AI Outfitter personas, rooted in Lean Start-up principles, define specific user roles with role, biography, and organization to guide the agent in reviewing design changes, code, and documentation from a targeted perspective. This ensures projects address clear human needs (e.g., engineers versus non-technical finance professionals) rather than amorphous audiences, supporting human-first design philosophy.

## Key points
- AI Outfitter personas are user role definitions with role, biography, and organization (e.g., John Doe as a designer at ACMICO)
- They enable the agent to review design, code, and documentation from a specific persona's perspective
- Without the Outfitter tool, personas function as project steering documents to clarify target audiences
- This approach avoids amorphous solutions by focusing on specific user roles (e.g., engineers vs. non-technical finance professionals)
