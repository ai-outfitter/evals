## Summary
The speaker proposes using **A_I_ Outfitter personas** as lightweight “project‑steering” documents that define the person (role, organization) a product is being built for. These personas act as a review checkpoint—letting humans and the AI jointly evaluate design changes, code, or documentation by answering how they appear to the intended persona—not merely as UI “outfitters.” Their purpose is to embed human‑first focus into every agent step of development.

## Key points
- A persona is a simple entity (often “agents dot M_D”) that captures who the product serves.  
- It includes a **role**, a **biography**, and an **organisation**; roles can switch between companies and projects.  
- The tool enables quick, consistent reviews of design, code, or docs from the persona’s viewpoint.  
- Personas function as *project‑steering* guides that keep the human and the agent aligned early on.  
- This approach supports a philosophy where “we design for humans first.”  
- Without such personas, developers risk writing generic blobs rather than specific audience‑oriented content.
