## Summary
AI Outfitter personas enable teams to review design, code, and documentation changes from the perspective of a specific audience. Adapted from lean startup methodology, personas function as "project steering docs" that help both humans and agents understand who they're building for, guiding decisions about technical level and communication style throughout the entire project lifecycle—from spec writing through planning to review.

## Key points
- Personas allow reviewing design changes, code, and documentation from a specific audience perspective
- Personas are flat, reusable files (like agents.md) describing role, biography, and organization
- The same person can have different roles and organizational contexts (e.g., John Doe as designer at ACME or researcher at Weyland)
- Personas function as project steering docs that help clarify who the product is for
- The design philosophy prioritizes humans first, with agents integrated to support that focus
- Personas help resolve ambiguous audience questions (e.g., engineer vs. finance person)
- Personas can be applied across all stages of the agentic process: specs, planning, review
- Using personas provides specificity rather than designing for a generic, "amorphous blob" audience
