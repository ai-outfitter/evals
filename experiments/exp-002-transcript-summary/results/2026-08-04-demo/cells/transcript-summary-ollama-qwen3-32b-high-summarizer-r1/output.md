## Summary
AI Outfitter personas, inspired by lean start-up principles, provide a framework for designing agents and projects by simulating specific user roles (e.g., "designer at ACMICO" or "researcher at Weyland"). Personas act as project steering documents to anchor design decisions and technical complexity around a concrete, human-centric identity rather than abstract requirements. This approach emphasizes human-first design with agents as collaborators, using personas to align both the human creator and the agent throughout the planning and review process.

## Key points
- A persona typically includes a role, biography, and organization to create a relatable, context-rich identity.
- Personas act as project steering documents to clarify the target audience and technical tone.
- The approach is rooted in lean start-up methodology but adapts it for agent-assisted development.
- Enables practical reviews of code, documentation, or design from the persona's perspective.
- Emphasizes designing for specific humans (e.g., engineers vs. finance personnel) instead of vague audience "blobs."
